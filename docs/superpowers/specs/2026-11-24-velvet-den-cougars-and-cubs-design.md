## 1. Intent & Why

**The Velvet Den: Cougars & Cubs** (working title, configurable in a single brand config file) is a cheeky, Vice-Sunset pop-art browser dating & vetting game paired with an automated lifestyle brand hub and community engine.

It serves the fast-growing **Cougar/Cub** age-gap community (`r/CougarsAndCubs` at 209K+ members) and curious newcomers who love sharp, trope-savvy relationship comedy. Mainstream dating sims either ignore older-woman/younger-man relationships or reduce them to explicit porn tropes that alienate actual cougars and trigger Stripe/PayPal bans. By centering the *real* insider humor of the lifestyle—zooming in with shock on *"Age: 26"* on dating apps, spotting the liquor-bottle photo trap right after a hot gym pic, keeping a cub on a playful "short leash" during a brutal 6 AM trail run, testing if he picks up the tapas bill (*Cougar ≠ Sugar Mama*), balancing chivalry with post-MeToo/Title IX Gen-Z sensibilities, earning playful "houseboy" choreplay behind fade-to-black doors, and navigating real heartbreaks like the kids/fertility mismatch—the game becomes an instant insider favorite that monetizes cleanly through 0%-fee tips, impulse Action Credit/VIP upsells, and curated lifestyle affiliate picks.

## 2. Goals, Non-Goals & Anti-Goals

### Goals
1. **Instant-Play Browser Game on GitHub Pages**: Zero-login, mobile-first responsive web game hosted free on a new `clubhost` GitHub repository via GitHub Pages.
2. **Interactive Wildlife Field Guide (Taxonomy Encyclopedia)**: Playfully teaches players the community's age-tier feline scale and younger-partner archetypes, unlocking collectible dossier stamps as players play:
   - **Lynx** (22–29) & **Puma** (30–39 — official `r/CougarsAndCubs` under-40 term)
   - **Cougar** (40–49, 10+ year gap)
   - **Jaguar** (50–59, apex confidence)
   - **Panther / Sabertooth** (60+, legendary icon)
   - **Cub** sub-species (*Golden Retriever Houseboy*, *Trail-Runner Himbo*, *Old-Soul Gentleman*, *Ambitious Founder/Grad*, vs. *The `"hey"` DM Goblin* & *Bottle-Service Frat Boy*)
   - Bonus lore entries: **Kitten** (younger woman in an age-gap match) and **Mountain Lion / Silver Fox** (older male counterparts).
3. **Dual-POV + Mix-and-Match Pairing Mixer**:
   - Choose **Play as Her (Vet & Train)** or **Play as Him (Impress & Commit)**.
   - Mix and match Her tier/life situation (e.g., *45F Done-With-Kids C-Suite Cougar*, *43F "Treat Me Like a Princess" Soft-Life Cougar* who spent 15 years carrying everyone's mental load and wants pure pampering/flowers/chivalry, *52F Trail-Running Empty-Nester Jaguar*, *36F Newly Divorced Cool-Aunt Puma*) with His age/archetype (e.g., *26M Wants-Kids Old-Soul Cub*, *24M High-Energy Houseboy Cub*, *28M Chivalrous Grad Cub*).
   - Automatically computes the age gap, **generational matchup** (e.g., *Gen-X Cougar × Gen-Z Cub*, *Elder Millennial Puma × Gen-Z Cub*, *Gen-X Jaguar × Millennial Cub*), official classification badge (e.g., *"19-Year Cougar/Cub Dynamic"* vs *"Puma Alert"*), and routes bespoke generational dialogue hooks + Act III subplot conflicts.
   - **Generational Code-Switching Mechanic ("Brain-to-Mouth Translator")**:
     - Cubs must actively **code-switch** to court across generations—suppressing raw Gen-Z group-chat brainrot (`"ngl tonight was fire 💀 u tryna link?"`, crew-sock streetwear, filming entrées, panicking at an unannounced phone call) without swinging into stiff try-hard cosplay, landing smoothly on tailored effort, real reservations, and direct communication.
     - Captures specific **Gen-X vs. Millennial vs. Gen-Z** tells:
       - *Gen-X Cougar/Jaguar (46–60+)*: Hates all-day micro-texting at work, loves a confident 6 PM phone call, fiercely independent, zero patience for therapy-speak excuses.
       - *Elder Millennial Puma/Cougar (34–45)*: Uses `😂` unironically, notices crew socks vs. ankle socks, panics if you call without texting first, loves a Google Calendar reservation link + emotional literacy.
       - *Millennial Cub (29–36)* vs. *Gen-Z Cub (21–28)*: Craft-IPA/brunch fluency vs. post-Title IX consent fluency + hiding TikTok slang (`"cooked"`, `"unc"`) in front of her 44-year-old friends.
4. **Act I — Dating App Swipe & Red-Flag Magnifier ("The Prowl")**:
   - Multi-photo dating app profile deck where players inspect photos, prompts, and badges before swiping:
     - **Shock-Value Age Zoom**: Tapping or revealing `"Age: 26"` triggers a dramatic comic zoom-in panel and internal monologue (*"Wait—he was learning multiplication tables during the housing crash?!"*).
     - **Photo Carousel Trap**: Photo 1 is an enticing, sun-drenched trail or tailored-suit shot; swiping to Photo 2 reveals him clutching a handle of cheap liquor in a LED-strip apartment (*Maturity Doubt* alert!).
     - **Profile Red/Beige/Green Flags**: Calling out `"Looking for: Short-term, open to long"` (*"Translation: literally everyone on this app"*), `Interests: Nightlife & Bottle Service` vs. `Trail Running & Home Cooking`, and filtering opening DMs (`"hey"` or uninvited `"mommy"` = instant left-swipe/archive).
5. **Act II — Core Dual-POV Episodic Scenes (Vetting, Training & Thrill)**:
   - Tracked by a 4-stat HUD + 3-strike **Red Flag Meter**:
     - **Maturity** (emotional regulation, financial self-sufficiency, follow-through)
     - **Chivalry** (planning reservations, opening doors, splitting/covering dates within budget)
     - **Gen-Z EQ** (post-MeToo/Title IX consent fluency, active listening, zero toxic ego)
     - **Chemistry** (banter, trail-run stamina, playful leash/houseboy tension)
   - Core trope episodes adaptable to both POVs:
     1. **The Check-Drop Test**: Cocktail/tapas bill lands on the table; navigating *Cougar ≠ Sugar Mama* pride, reciprocity, and effortless chivalry.
     2. **6 AM Ridge Trail Run ("Short Leash & Stamina Check")**: Putting his boasted 20-something cardio to the test on her steep Saturday trail run, complete with cheeky *"stay on my pace / short leash"* banter.
     3. **Gen-Z Fluency Meets Classic Courtship**: Handling public side-eyes, music/pop-culture generation gaps, enthusiastic consent check-ins, and bold romance.
     4. **Houseboy Sunday (Earned Fade-to-Black Reward)**: Unlocked only when *Maturity* and *Gen-Z EQ* clear the bar—linen apron choreplay, fixing things around her place, pampering, and cheeky dominant/nurturing hints that fade tastefully to black.
6. **Act III — Authentic Community Subplots (Commitment Boss Fight)**:
   - Dynamically selected based on the Pairing Mixer life-situation tags:
     - **The Kids / Fertility Crossroads**: She's already raised kids (or can't/won't have more); he loves her deeply but thought he wanted kids someday. Multiple nuanced endings (*All-In Child-Free Partnership*, *Loving Bittersweet Parting*, *Wishful Denial Fumble*).
     - **Meeting Her 22-Year-Old Kid & Skeptical Brunch Friends**: Surviving brunch when her college-senior daughter is two years younger than the Cub, or meeting *his* mom who graduated high school the same year she did.

### Non-Goals (v1)
- Backend databases, user login/passwords, or server-hosted state.
- Live LLM API calls (avoids recurring token cost and Stripe AI adult-content compliance risk).
- Real-person matchmaking/chatting inside the app.

### Anti-Goals (Hard Red Lines)
- **Zero explicit NSFW scenes, nudity, or adult-toy storefront links** (must remain PG-13/R-rated cheeky comedy so Stripe and Ko-fi accounts are 100% safe).
- **Zero automated posting or bot scraping targeting `r/CougarsAndCubs` or third-party subreddits** (prevents subreddit bans and domain blacklists).
- **Zero Rockstar / Take-Two IP infringement** (no `GTA` name, no `Pricedown` font, no Rockstar stars, no traced box-art layouts).
- **Zero unironic creepy fetishization**: Opening with crude fetish lines triggers comedic rejection, staying true to actual community values.

## 3. Constraints

- **Hosting**: Static single-page web application deployable via GitHub Actions to **GitHub Pages** (`https://clubhost.github.io/<repo-name>/` with relative Vite `base` support).
- **Payment Compliance**: All text, SVG illustrations, and external affiliate links must comply with Stripe's Prohibited & Restricted Businesses policy and Ko-fi's Content Guidelines (§1 & §2.2).
- **IP Cleanliness**: Pop-art comic cel-shading, angled mosaic split-panels, sunset neon coral/teal/gold gradients, and bold geometric display fonts (`Bebas Neue` / `Anton` + `Plus Jakarta Sans` via Google Fonts or system fallbacks) evoke sun-drenched crime/romance satire purely through uncopyrightable genre style.
- **UX**: Full playability on mobile portrait viewports (375px+) and desktop widescreen without horizontal scroll.

## 4. Approaches Considered

1. **Approach A — Zero-Backend Static Web App on GitHub Pages + Payment Links + GitHub Actions Owned-Channel Bot (Selected)**
   - *Summary*: Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind SPA with `localStorage` state/daily credits, configurable Stripe Payment Links + VIP unlock codes/return tokens, Ko-fi tip drawer, JSON-driven affiliate shop, Canvas PNG receipt export, and GitHub Actions daily Discord/owned-sub dilemma poster.
   - *Trade-offs*: \$0/month hosting, zero backend maintenance, instant launch. Local credit storage isn't cryptographically tamper-proof against DevTools edits, which is an ideal trade-off for a frictionless indie browser game monetizing tips, impulse VIP passes, and affiliates.
2. **Approach B — Serverless Crowd-Stats App (Cloudflare Pages/Workers + KV)**
   - *Summary*: Adds serverless state for Stripe webhooks and global choice percentages.
   - *Why not chosen for v1*: Adds Cloudflare + webhook deployment setup before launch; v1 architecture keeps state modular so this can be bolted on later.
3. **Approach C — AI-Hybrid App (LLM Freeform DM Vetting)**
   - *Summary*: Adds live LLM chat rounds graded by an AI Cougar/Cub.
   - *Why not chosen for v1*: Introduces recurring token costs and risk of user-prompted explicit text violating Stripe's AI content rules.
